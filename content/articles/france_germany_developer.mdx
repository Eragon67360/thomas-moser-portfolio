## A border I've been crossing since my studies
Working in Germany didn't come out of nowhere. My path has zigzagged across three countries:

- **Haguenau, France:** a DUT in electrical engineering and industrial computing (2018 to 2019).
- **Offenburg, Germany:** a bachelor in electrical engineering and information technology at the Fachhochschule (2019 to 2021).
- **Neuchâtel, Switzerland:** a bachelor of engineering in computer science and communication systems at the Haute-École ARC (2021 to 2022).

Both of my bachelor's theses were done abroad too: image processing on FPGA at SwissTiming in Switzerland, then a Python test framework at BMG LABTECH in Ortenberg, a few kilometres from Offenburg, where I then worked as a software developer.
