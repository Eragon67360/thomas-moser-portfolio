## Introduction
Today I'm a full-stack developer: websites, admin dashboards, APIs, mobile apps. But my studies started a long way from the browser, in electrical engineering, and my first bachelor's thesis was about image processing on an FPGA.

This post is about the path between the two: what each step was, and how I ended up on the web.

## Starting with electronics
My studies began with a DUT in electrical engineering and industrial computing at the IUT de Haguenau (2018 to 2019). I then did a bachelor in electrical engineering and information technology at the Fachhochschule Offenburg (2019 to 2021), and a bachelor of engineering in computer science and communication systems at the Haute-École ARC in Neuchâtel (2021 to 2022).

## The FPGA thesis
From April to July 2022, I did my bachelor's thesis at SwissTiming in Corgémont, Switzerland: image processing on an FPGA, with the accompanying software written in C++.

An FPGA is a chip you program by describing hardware rather than writing instructions: you decide which logic exists and how it is wired, so an image pipeline can process pixels in parallel, clock cycle by clock cycle.

## Moving up, one step at a time
Looking at my path afterwards, each job moved a little further away from the hardware:

- **BMG LABTECH, Ortenberg (Sep 2022 to Mar 2023):** my second bachelor's thesis, a Python framework with a graphical interface to test the accuracy of data acquired with the MARS software against reference datasets.
- **BMG LABTECH (May to Sep 2023):** .NET (C#) software for the PC interface of the company's microplate reader, in a team of eight developers.
- **MOCA (Oct 2023 to Jul 2024):** a micro-enterprise with the designer Cristina Andrés. My first client was Curefab Technologies in Munich, whose company website I built with ConcreteCMS and PHP.
- **avenit group, Offenburg (since Jul 2024):** Vue.js and NestJS projects and TYPO3 websites at avenit, then web development at Vusyon since September 2025.

In parallel, I build my own projects end to end: this site in Next.js, a website, an admin and a Flutter app for the music association I play in, and a few others you can find on the [projects page](/projects).
